# 12: Bonus: PDF Invoice

**What to build:** From the Invoice detail page, users who can view Invoices download `INV-0001.pdf`. It shows:
- Siam Bean's name, address and Tax ID
- the Customer's name, address and Tax ID
- the Invoice number, date and Due Date
- the lines (SKU, name, quantity with unit, Unit Price, amount)
- Subtotal, VAT 7% and Total as `9,630.00 THB`, plus Paid/Balance

It's generated by the API, so the numbers come from the stored Invoice.
