# 12: Bonus: PDF Invoice

**What to build:** From the Invoice detail page, users who can view Invoices download `INV-0001.pdf`. It shows:
- Siam Bean's name, address and Tax ID
- the Customer's name, address and Tax ID
- the Invoice number, date and Due Date
- the lines (SKU, name, quantity with unit, Unit Price, amount)
- Subtotal, VAT 7% and Total as `9,630.00 THB`, plus Paid/Balance

It's generated by the API, so the numbers come from the stored Invoice.

**Blocked by:** 10

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Branch:** `feature/pdf-invoice`

- [ ] `GET /api/invoices/{id}/pdf` as Sales, Accountant or Admin → `200 application/pdf`, with a filename containing the Invoice number. Warehouse → `403`.
- [ ] The PDF text contains `INV-0001`, `9,000.00`, `630.00`, `9,630.00` and both Tax IDs (checked in a test by extracting the text).
- [ ] Changing the Product Price afterwards doesn't change the PDF.
- [ ] The "Download PDF" button works on desktop and on a phone.
